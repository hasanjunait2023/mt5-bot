## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses

**Weaknesses**:

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Restrict trading sessions**: Focus on **London (07:00-12:00 UTC)** or **New York overlap (12:00-16:00 UTC)** where EURUSD volatility is higher.

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **High risk of ruin**: 62% of trades lose money, with losses exceeding wins in both frequency and size.  
- **Equity curve instability**: Multiple consecutive losses (e.g., October 2025: 4 SLs in a row).  
- **Max drawdown (9.8%)**: Relatively low but irrelevant given the net loss.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Disable strategy** or **optimize further with strict filters** (e.g., session-based rules, improved entry logic). Prioritize reducing losing trades before retesting.
