# Backtest Analysis: ICT 2022 — EURUSD  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Unprofitable**  
The strategy generated a **-4.6% return** with a **profit factor of 0.92** (losses > wins), indicating systematic inefficiency.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- **Larger average wins** ($13.47) vs. losses ($9.07), suggesting potential in favorable conditions.  
- **Moderate max drawdown** (9.8%), indicating risk management is not excessively aggressive.  
- **Periodic profitability** in months like June 2025 (+$51.90) and October 2025 (+$58.95).  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **Low win rate** (38.2%) and **more losses than wins** (63 losses vs. 39 wins).  
- **Frequent stop-loss triggers** with 0-pip losses (e.g., multiple "SL" exits at entry price), suggesting overly tight or poorly placed stops.  
- **Negative overall equity** (-$45.95) despite favorable average win/loss ratio.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Adjust stop-loss levels**: Increase SL distance to avoid immediate triggers (e.g., from 0 pips to 10–15 pips).  
- **Session filtering**: Restrict trading to **London session (07:00–12:00 UTC)** or **New York overlap (12:00–16:00 UTC)** where volatility and momentum may improve edge.  
- **Optimize take-profit**: Extend TP levels (e.g., from current ~10–15 pips to 15–20 pips) to capitalize on stronger trends observed in winning trades.  
- **Lot size adjustment**: Reduce lot sizes for high-frequency trades to mitigate cumulative losses.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Drawdown risk**: Moderate (9.8% max drawdown), but compounded by consistent losses.  
- **Survivability risk**: High due to negative equity trajectory; requires immediate optimization to avoid depletion of capital.  
- **Execution risk**: Frequent 0-pip SL losses suggest slippage or poor entry/exit logic.
