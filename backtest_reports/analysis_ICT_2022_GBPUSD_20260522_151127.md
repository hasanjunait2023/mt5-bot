## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable**  
The strategy generated a +53.1% return over 2.4 years with a strong profit factor (1.87) and low max drawdown (8.9%).  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths:**  
- **Consistent Profitability**: 10/14 months positive, with standout gains in Oct 2025 (+$182.95) and Feb/Mar 2026.  
- **Favorable Risk-Reward**: Avg. win ($15.39) > Avg. loss ($9.97), supported by a 1.87 profit factor.  
- **Low Drawdown**: Max drawdown of 8.9% indicates controlled risk.  

**Weaknesses:**  
- **Frequent Stop Loss Triggers**: 61 losses vs. 74 wins (44% loss rate), suggesting over-trading or tight stops.  
- **Inefficient Trade Clustering**: Multiple same-direction trades on the same day (e.g., 2025-01-29, 2025-02-06) may indicate over-optimistic entry logic.  
- **Stagnant Periods**: March 2025 (-$0.14) and Dec 2025 ($0.00) show minimal returns.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Restriction**: Limit trades to **London session (07:00–12:00 UTC)** to focus on high-liquidity hours (many winning trades clustered here).  
- **Stop Loss Adjustment**: Increase SL distance by 20–30% (e.g., from ~10 pips to ~13 pips) to reduce false breakouts.  
- **Profit Target Optimization**: Add **trailing stop** on TP2 to capture extended trends (e.g., 2025-09-05, 2025-10-09).  
- **Position Sizing**: Cap lot size at **0.2** to reduce exposure in volatile conditions.

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Low-Moderate Risk**: Max drawdown (8.9%) and avg. loss ($9.97) are manageable for a retail account.  
- **Over-Optimization Risk**: High number of trades (135) in 2.4 years may lead to curve-fitting; validate on out-of-sample data.  
- **Market Dependency**: Strong performance in 2025–2026 GBPUSD trends; test against range-bound or high-volatility regimes.
