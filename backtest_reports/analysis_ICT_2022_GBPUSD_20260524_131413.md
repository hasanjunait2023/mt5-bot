# Backtest Analysis Report: ICT 2022 — GBPUSD  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable**  
The strategy generated a **53.1% return** with a **profit factor of 1.87** and a **max drawdown of 8.9%**, indicating consistent profitability despite moderate risk.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths:**  
- **High Profit Factor (1.87):** Wins significantly outsize losses.  
- **Strong Average Win ($15.39):** Larger gains compensate for frequent smaller losses.  
- **Upward Equity Trend:** Steady growth over 2+ years, with standout months like October 2025 (+$182.95).  

**Weaknesses:**  
- **Frequent Stop Losses:** ~45% of trades exited at SL (zero profit), suggesting over-trading or tight risk parameters.  
- **Inconsistent Monthly Performance:** March 2025 (-$0.14) and December 2025 ($0.00) show periods of stagnation.  
- **Losing Trades (61/135):** Slightly below 55% win rate, indicating room for entry/exit refinement.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Restriction:** Restrict trading to **London session (07:00–12:00 UTC)** to focus on high-liquidity periods (GBPUSD often trends strongly here).  
- **Lot Size Optimization:** Cap lot size at **0.2** to reduce risk per trade (larger lots like 0.34 occasionally led to higher losses).  
- **TP/SL Adjustment:** Extend TP by **2–3 pips** to capture more profitable exits (e.g., 13-pip wins could become 15-pip wins).  
- **Filter Losing Conditions:** Avoid trading during low volatility (e.g., weekends, holidays) and minor news events.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Drawdown Risk:** Max drawdown (8.9%) is acceptable for FX trading but could be improved by reducing position size or tightening SL.  
- **Overfitting Risk:** Strategy may be over-optimized for this dataset; validate on out-of-sample data.  
- **Dependency Risk:** Heavy reliance on London session performance—monitor geopolitical/events impacting GBP.
