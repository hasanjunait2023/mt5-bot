# Backtest Analysis Report: ICT 2022 — GBPUSD  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable**  
The strategy generated a +53.1% return over 2.5 years with a profit factor of 1.87 and a maximum drawdown of 8.9%, indicating consistent profitability with controlled risk.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths:**  
- **High Profit Factor (1.87):** Wins significantly outsize losses.  
- **Consistent Monthly Gains:** 14/17 months positive, with strong performance in 2026.  
- **Favorable Risk-Reward:** Avg. win ($15.39) > Avg. loss ($9.97).  

**Weaknesses:**  
- **Frequent Stop-Loss Triggers:** Multiple trades exited at SL with $0 P&L (e.g., repeated SL hits in April–May 2025).  
- **March 2025 Underperformance:** Near-zero return despite active trading.  
- **Session Dependency:** Mixed results across different times (e.g., SELL trades in July 2025 showed SL losses vs. TP gains).  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Restriction:** Restrict trading to **London session (07:00–12:00 UTC)** where volatility and liquidity are highest for GBPUSD.  
- **Stop-Loss Adjustment:** Increase SL distance by **3–5 pips** to avoid premature exits in choppy markets.  
- **Position Sizing:** Cap lot size at **0.2** to reduce exposure in high-drawdown periods.  
- **Time Filter:** Avoid trading during **low-liquidity hours** (e.g., 22:00–04:00 UTC) based on trade log timing patterns.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Drawdown Risk:** Max drawdown (8.9%) is acceptable for a trending strategy but could be tightened with SL adjustments.  
- **Overfitting Risk:** Strategy performed well in 2026 but requires validation on out-of-sample data.  
- **Market Dependency:** Performance may degrade in ranging markets due to frequent SL triggers.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize Further**  
Refine parameters (session filter, SL/TP adjustments) and re-backtest on out-of-sample data before considering deployment.  

---

**SUMMARY:** Profitable strategy with strong risk-reward but needs optimization for SL sensitivity and session timing before live deployment.
