# Backtest Analysis Report: ICT 2022 — XAUUSD  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable**  
The strategy generated a **302.1% return** with a **profit factor of 2.11** and **12.0% max drawdown**, indicating strong risk-adjusted returns.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- High profit factor (2.11) and win rate (67.4%).  
- Consistent monthly profits except for minor losses in March 2025 (-$0.33) and May 2026 (-$50.47).  
- Average win ($47.06) slightly exceeds average loss ($46.10), leveraging compounding.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- Occasional clusters of losing trades (e.g., consecutive SL hits in December 2024, February 2025).  
- Some months show volatility in performance (e.g., March 2025 near-zero P&L).  
- Potential overtrading (181 trades in 2.5 years).  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Restriction**: Focus on **London (07:00-12:00 UTC)** and **New York (12:00-17:00 UTC)** sessions where volatility and liquidity are higher.  
- **Lot Sizing**: Implement **dynamic lot sizing** (e.g., 2% risk per trade) to scale position sizes with account growth.  
- **Stop-Loss/Take-Profit**: Adjust SL to **1.5x ATR(14)** to reduce false breakouts; keep TP at 2:1 risk-reward ratio.  
- **Trade Filtering**: Add a **cooldown period of 1 hour** between trades to avoid overtrading.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Max Drawdown**: 12% is acceptable for a trending strategy but requires monitoring during high-impact news events.  
- **Risk-Reward**: Positive (2.11 profit factor), but SL efficiency (~32.4% of trades hit SL) suggests room for improvement.  
- **Liquidity Risk**: XAUUSD is a major pair, so execution should not be a concern.  

---

**Optimize Further**  
Refine parameters (session filters, dynamic lot sizing, SL adjustments) and retest. If robustness improves, proceed to **small-scale deployment** with tight risk limits.
