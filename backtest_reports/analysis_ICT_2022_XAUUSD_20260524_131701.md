# Backtest Analysis: ICT 2022 — XAUUSD  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable**  
The strategy generated a **302.1% return** with a **profit factor of 2.11** and **67.4% win rate**, indicating strong performance. Max drawdown of **12%** is relatively low for the returns achieved.  

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  
**Strengths**:  
- High profit factor (2.11) and return (302.1%).  
- Consistent monthly profitability (only 2 losing months in 27).  
- Strong average win ($47.06) vs. average loss ($46.10).  

**Weaknesses**:  
- Occasional large losing months (e.g., May 2026: -$50.47).  
- Some trades show tight SL/TP (e.g., SL hits near TP).  
- Potential overtrading (multiple similar entries in short timeframes).  

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Restriction**: Focus on **London (07:00-16:00 UTC)** and **New York (12:00-20:00 UTC)** sessions for higher liquidity.  
- **Position Sizing**: Cap lot size at **1.0** to reduce risk from large trades (e.g., 2.11 lots in one trade).  
- **SL/TP Adjustment**: Increase TP by **10-15 pips** to capture more profits; tighten SL to **30-40 pips** to reduce losses.  
- **Cooldown Period**: Implement a **15-minute cooldown** between trades to avoid overtrading.  

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Max Drawdown**: 12% is acceptable given the high returns.  
- **Risk-Reward Balance**: Near-equal average win/loss suggests room to improve risk-reward ratio.  
- **Liquidity Dependence**: Strategy may underperform in low-liquidity conditions (e.g., holidays).  

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize Further**  
Refine SL/TP, test session restrictions, and validate cooldown parameters. Re-backtest before deployment.
