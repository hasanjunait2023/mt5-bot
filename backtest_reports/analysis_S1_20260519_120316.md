# Backtest Analysis Report: Swing Scalp Strategy  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable**  
The strategy generated a **+140.8% return** over 2.5 years with a **profit factor of 1.19**, indicating consistent edge despite volatility.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths:**  
- **Positive expectancy**: Avg. win ($15.06) > avg. loss ($13.26).  
- **Recent performance**: Strong gains in 2026 (e.g., +$364.06 in May 2026).  
- **High trade frequency**: 1,160 trades in 2.5 years (consistent opportunity capture).  

**Weaknesses:**  
- **High drawdown**: 19.9% max drawdown suggests significant risk exposure.  
- **Loss clusters**: Multiple consecutive losing months (e.g., Jan–Feb 2025, May 2025).  
- **Aggressive stop-loss**: 60-pip SL dominates losses, eroding profits during volatile periods.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Restrict trading hours**: Focus on **London session (07:00–12:00 UTC)** where volatility and liquidity are highest.  
- **Tighten stop-loss**: Reduce SL from 60 pips to **20–30 pips** to align with scalping logic.  
- **Dynamic position sizing**: Cap lot size at **0.3** to limit risk per trade.  
- **Add partial take-profit**: Implement **TP1 at 10 pips (50% exit)** and TP2 at 20 pips to secure profits early.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Drawdown risk**: 19.9% is acceptable for aggressive strategies but requires strict risk management.  
- **Over-leveraged losses**: Avg. loss ($13.26) is 87% of avg. win, indicating SL needs adjustment.  
- **Transaction costs**: High trade frequency may amplify slippage/spread impacts (not accounted for in results).
