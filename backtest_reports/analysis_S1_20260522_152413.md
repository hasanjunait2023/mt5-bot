# Backtest Analysis: S1 — Swing Scalp  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Unprofitable**  
The strategy generated a final balance of **$-3,106.93** (-410.7% return) with a profit factor of **0.62** and a max drawdown of **422.5%**, indicating severe losses outweighing gains.  

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  
**Strengths**:  
- Occasional profitability in specific months (e.g., +$190.80 in 2024-10, +$349.44 in 2026-02).  
- Frequent small wins ($14.35 avg) suggest potential in tight scalping setups.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **Low win rate (40.4%)** and **large average losses (-$15.70)** vs. smaller wins.  
- **Excessive drawdown** (422.5%) and consistent negative months (e.g., -2026-05: -$55.36).  
- Overly aggressive lot sizing and poor risk-reward ratio (avg loss > avg win).  
- Heavy reliance on **BUY orders** (94% of trades), likely failing in downtrends.  

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Restrict trading sessions**: Focus on **London (07:00-12:00 UTC)** and **New York overlap (12:00-16:00 UTC)** where volatility may favor scalping.  
- **Adjust stop-loss**: Increase SL from 60 pips to **20-30 pips** to reduce impact of large losses.  
- **Cap lot size**: Limit to **0.1-0.2 lots** to reduce risk per trade.  
- **Add trend filter**: Only take trades in the direction of the 200-period EMA.  

## 4. Risk Assessment  
**High Risk**  
- Max drawdown exceeds 400%, eroding the entire account.  
- High loss frequency (68% of trades) and inconsistent performance across months.  
- Lack of diversification in trade direction (mostly longs).  

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Disable or Optimize Further**  
Immediately halt live deployment. Prioritize:  
1. Backtest with adjusted SL/TP and session filters.  
2. Test trend-following logic to avoid counter-trend trades.  
3. Re-evaluate lot sizing and risk management rules.
