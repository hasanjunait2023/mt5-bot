# Backtest Analysis: S1 — Swing Scalp  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Unprofitable**  
The strategy generated a **-410.7% return** with a final balance of **-$3,106.93**, indicating severe losses. The profit factor of **0.62** (losses exceeding gains) and **40.4% win rate** confirm consistent underperformance.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- Occasional monthly profitability (e.g., +$190.80 in 2024-10, +$349.44 in 2026-02).  
- Some winning trades with small pip gains (+2 pips).  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **High negative skew**: Avg. loss ($-15.70) > Avg. win ($14.35).  
- **Excessive drawdown**: 422.5% max drawdown, eroding capital rapidly.  
- **Poor risk-reward**: 60-pip stop-loss vs. 2-pip take-profit in most trades.  
- **Inconsistent performance**: 11 out of 23 months showed losses.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Adjust stop-loss/take-profit**: Increase take-profit to at least **3:1 risk-reward ratio** (e.g., 60-pip SL → 180-pip TP).  
- **Session filtering**: Restrict trading to **London session (07:00-12:00 UTC)** where volatility may favor scalping.  
- **Lot size control**: Cap lot size at **0.2** to reduce exposure per trade.  
- **Filter entry conditions**: Avoid trading during low-liquidity periods (e.g., Asian session).  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Extreme drawdown risk**: 422.5% max drawdown suggests poor risk management.  
- **Capital depletion**: Strategy risks complete capital loss within months.  
- **Over-leveraged**: High lot sizes relative to account size exacerbate losses.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize further** with adjusted SL/TP ratios, session filters, and tighter entry rules. If retesting fails to improve profit factor above **1.2** and reduce drawdown below **20%**, **disable** the strategy.  

---  

SUMMARY: Strategy unprofitable with extreme drawdowns and poor risk-reward. Optimize SL/TP, session timing, and lot sizing before retesting; otherwise, disable.
