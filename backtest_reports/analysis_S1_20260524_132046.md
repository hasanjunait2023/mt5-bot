# Backtest Analysis: S1 — Swing Scalp  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Unprofitable**  
The strategy generated a **-410.7% return** with a final balance of **$-3,106.93** (initial: $1,000), indicating severe losses.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  
**Strengths**:  
- Occasional monthly profitability (e.g., 2024-10: +$190.80, 2026-05: +$382.13).  
- Frequent small wins (avg. +$14.35) when trades succeed.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **Low win rate (40.4%)** and **profit factor (0.62)**, signaling more losses than gains.  
- **Lopsided risk-reward**: Avg. loss (-$15.70) exceeds avg. win (+$14.35).  
- **Extreme max drawdown (422.5%)**, eroding capital rapidly.  
- **Inconsistent performance**: 12 out of 26 months showed net losses.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Restrict trading hours**: Focus on **London session (07:00-12:00 UTC)** or **New York overlap (12:00-16:00 UTC)** where volatility may improve edge.  
- **Adjust risk-reward ratio**: Increase take-profit (TP) to **at least 1:1.5 risk-reward** (e.g., TP=+90 pips vs. SL=-60 pips).  
- **Tighten stop-loss**: Reduce SL to **-30 pips** to limit per-trade losses.  
- **Filter entries**: Avoid trading during low-liquidity periods (e.g., Asian session outside major news).  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **High risk of ruin**: Max drawdown exceeds 400%, far beyond acceptable thresholds (typically <30%).  
- **Negative expectancy**: Losses consistently outweigh gains, with a **-410.7% return** over 2.5 years.  
- **Over-leveraged**: Avg. loss size (-$15.70) relative to account size suggests excessive position sizing.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Disable strategy immediately**. The backtest shows systemic flaws (poor risk-reward, high drawdown) that make it unsustainable. Prioritize:  
1. Re-evaluate entry/exit logic and risk parameters.  
2. Test optimized settings on a smaller dataset before re-deployment.  

---

**SUMMARY:** Unprofitable strategy with -410.7% return, 40.4% win rate, and 422.5% max drawdown. Disable and re-optimize risk-reward parameters before further testing.
