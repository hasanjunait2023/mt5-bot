## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- Occasional positive months (e.g., +$382.13 in 2026-05).  
- Frequent small wins (avg. +$14.35) when trades work.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **Low win rate (40.4%)** with **more losses (689) than wins (468)**.  
- **Lopsided risk-reward**: Avg. loss (-$15.70) > avg. win (+$14.35).  
- **Excessive stop-loss (SL) triggers** (60 pips) vs. tiny take-profit (TP ~2 pips).  
- **High drawdown (422.5%)** and inconsistent monthly performance.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Adjust risk-reward ratio**: Increase TP to at least 1:1 (e.g., 60 pips TP) or reduce SL to 20 pips.  
- **Restrict trading hours**: Focus on **London session (07:00-12:00 UTC)** or New York overlap for better liquidity.  
- **Fix lot sizing**: Use **fixed lots (e.g., 0.1–0.2)** instead of variable sizing to reduce risk.  
- **Filter entries**: Avoid trading during low volatility (e.g., Asian session) or major news events.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Extreme drawdown risk**: Max drawdown exceeds 400% of initial capital.  
- **Over-leveraged**: Avg. loss size (-$15.70) represents ~1.5% of initial $1,000 balance per trade.  
- **Compounding losses**: Variable lot sizing amplifies losses during drawdowns.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Disable strategy immediately**. The negative expectancy and poor risk-reward structure make it unsustainable. If optimization is pursued, prioritize:  
1. Rebalancing SL/TP ratios.  
2. Adding filters for market conditions (e.g., trends, volatility).
