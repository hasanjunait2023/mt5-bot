# Backtest Analysis Report: S1v2 — Swing Scalp  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable**  
The strategy generated a **6423.1% return** over 2.4 years with a **profit factor of 2.55**, indicating strong positive expectancy despite a near-breakeven win rate (49.7%).  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- **High Reward-to-Risk Ratio**: Avg Win ($183.62) > 2.5x Avg Loss ($71.25).  
- **Scalability**: Performance improves over time (e.g., $17,657.98 profit in May 2026).  
- **Consistent Profitability**: 11/21 months showed gains, with 7 months exceeding $300 in profit.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **High Drawdown**: 14.5% max drawdown suggests significant risk exposure.  
- **Inconsistent Early Performance**: Multiple losing months in 2024-2025 (e.g., -$66.23 in Sep 2024).  
- **Stop-Loss Clustering**: Frequent SL triggers (e.g., 5 consecutive SL losses in Jan 2026).  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Filter**: Restrict trading to **London session (07:00-12:00 UTC)** to capitalize on volatility (many winning trades clustered in this window).  
- **Stop-Loss Adjustment**: Increase SL distance from 60 pips to **80-100 pips** to reduce false breakouts.  
- **Position Sizing**: Cap lot size growth after $50k equity (later trades use >3.0 lots, increasing risk).  
- **Trailing Stop Optimization**: Tighten trailing stop logic after 50 pips profit to lock in gains.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Risk Level**: Medium-High  
  - Drawdowns are manageable relative to returns but could stress unprepared investors.  
  - Overexposure in later trades (large lots) amplifies risk.  
- **Liquidity Risk**: High lot sizes in 2026 may not be replicable in live markets without slippage.
