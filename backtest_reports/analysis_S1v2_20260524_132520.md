**Strengths**:

**Weaknesses**:

- **Trend-filter**: Add a **200-period EMA filter** to avoid counter-trend entries.

- **Over-leveraging**: Aggressive lot sizing in later trades (e.g., 3.76 lots) exacerbates volatility.  
- **Dependency on large wins**: Profitability relies heavily on occasional large trailing profits (e.g., +$2,195 in 2026-02-13).
