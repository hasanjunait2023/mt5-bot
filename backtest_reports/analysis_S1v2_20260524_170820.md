# Backtest Analysis: S1v2 — Swing Scalp  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable** — Exceptional net profit ($36,503.74) and return (+3650.4%) with a profit factor of 1.62. However, high drawdown and win rate concerns require mitigation.  

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  
**Strengths**:  
- **High Reward/Risk Ratio**: Avg Win ($184.38) > Avg Loss ($92.92).  
- **Scalability**: Strong compounding in later months (e.g., +$12k in 2026).  
- **Profit Factor**: 1.62 indicates consistent edge.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **High Drawdown**: 235.6% max drawdown suggests extreme volatility.  
- **Low Win Rate**: 44.9% win rate with more losses (637) than wins (519).  
- **Overfitting Risk**: Explosive gains in 2026 may reflect curve-fitting.  

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Filter**: Restrict trading to **London session (07:00–12:00 UTC)** to focus on high-liquidity periods (many winning trades clustered in active hours).  
- **Lot Size Cap**: Limit lots to **≤2.5** to reduce risk from aggressive position sizing in later trades.  
- **Stop Loss Adjustment**: Increase SL to **100 pips** to avoid frequent breakeven exits and reduce false stops.  
- **Time Filter**: Exclude trades during **low-volatility periods** (e.g., weekends, holidays) to improve signal quality.  

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Drawdown Risk**: 235.6% max drawdown is unsustainable; equity could be wiped out during live trading.  
- **Over-Optimization Risk**: Strategy may perform poorly out-of-sample due to reliance on 2026’s volatile market conditions.  
- **Liquidity Risk**: Large lot sizes in later trades may lead to slippage in real-market execution.  

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize Further** — Address drawdown and overfitting risks by:  
1. Implementing session/time filters.  
2. Testing fixed lot sizes or risk-based position sizing.  
3. Validating performance on out-of-sample data.  

---

SUMMARY: Profitable strategy with +3650% return but high drawdown (235.6%) and overfitting risks; optimize session filters, lot sizing, and SL before deployment.
