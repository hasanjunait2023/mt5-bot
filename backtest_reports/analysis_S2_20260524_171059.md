# Backtest Analysis: S2 — M5 Scalp  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Unprofitable**  
The strategy generated a **-433.8% return** with a **final balance of -$3,338.48** from a $1,000 starting capital. Key metrics like a **1.8% win rate**, **0.01 profit factor**, and **433.8% max drawdown** confirm consistent losses.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- **Small average win size**: $1.50 per winning trade (though insufficient to offset losses).  
- **Disciplined stop-loss**: Consistent use of tight stops (mostly -18 pips).  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **Extremely low win rate**: Only **1.8% of trades profitable** (18 wins vs. 1,007 losses).  
- **Poor risk-reward ratio**: Average loss ($-4.34) is **~3x larger** than average win.  
- **Frequent losing streaks**: 1025 total trades with **no profitable months** except Dec 2024 ($16.34 gain).  
- **Overtrading**: High trade frequency (1025 trades in 2.5 years) with minimal edge.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Restrict trading sessions**: Focus on **London (07:00-12:00 UTC)** or **New York (12:00-17:00 UTC)** where volatility might favor the strategy.  
- **Adjust risk-reward ratio**: Increase take-profit targets (e.g., +40 pips instead of +2 pips) to improve profitability.  
- **Filter entries**: Add price-action or volume filters to reduce false signals.  
- **Reduce position size**: Lower lot sizes (e.g., 0.05–0.10) to mitigate losses.  
- **Avoid choppy markets**: Implement a volatility filter (e.g., ATR-based) to skip low-trend periods.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Extreme drawdown risk**: Max drawdown of **433.8%** indicates the strategy could wipe out the account quickly.  
- **High trade frequency + low edge**: Combines rapid compounding of losses with no recovery mechanism.  
- **Liquidity risk**: Small lot sizes may not be the issue, but cumulative losses from overtrading are catastrophic.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Disable and Optimize Further**  
The strategy is fundamentally unprofitable in its current form. Prioritize:  
1. Re-evaluate entry/exit logic (e.g., tighter filters, dynamic stops).  
2. Test with adjusted risk-reward ratios and session restrictions.  
3. Validate on out-of-sample data before reconsidering deployment.  

---

SUMMARY: Strategy S2 — M5 Scalp is unprofitable with a 1.8% win rate, 0.01 profit factor, and 433.8% drawdown. Disable live trading and optimize entry rules, risk-reward ratios, and session timing.
