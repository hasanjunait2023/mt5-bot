## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations

- **Position Sizing**: Cap lot size at **1.5 lots** to mitigate risk from large trades (e.g., 1.79 lots in losing trades).  
- **Time Filter**: Avoid trading during low-liquidity periods (e.g., Asian session outside Tokyo momentum).
