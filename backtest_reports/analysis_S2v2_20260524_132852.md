**Strengths**:  
- High profit factor (2.57) and strong risk-reward ratio (avg win $336.48 vs avg loss $115.09).  
- Consistent monthly growth (e.g., +12176.67 in 2026-01).

**Weaknesses**:

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Restriction**: Focus on **London session (07:00-12:00 UTC)** for higher volatility and better trade efficiency.  
- **Stop-Loss Adjustment**: Increase SL distance slightly (e.g., 30 pips) to reduce false break-even triggers.  
- **Position Sizing**: Cap lot size at **1.5 lots** to mitigate risk during drawdowns.  
- **Time Filter**: Avoid trading during low-liquidity periods (e.g., weekends, holidays).  

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Max Drawdown**: 13.7% is acceptable given the high returns but requires strict risk management.  
- **Liquidity Risk**: High trade frequency may face slippage during news events.  
- **Overfitting Risk**: Strategy performance in 2026-05 (-1367.60) suggests sensitivity to market shifts.  

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Deploy with Monitoring**  
Go live with a small position size (e.g., 0.5 lots) and closely monitor performance during the first month. Optimize parameters if drawdown exceeds 15%.
