# Backtest Analysis: S4 — Multi-Pair Strategy  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Unprofitable**  
The strategy generated a **-16.6% return** with a **profit factor of 0.28**, indicating significant losses outweighing gains. The final balance decreased from $1,000 to $834, and the **max drawdown was 16.6%**.  

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  
**Strengths**:  
- Occasional winning trades with **+15 pips** (TP2 exits).  
- Small position sizing (lots of 0.01–0.08) limits per-trade risk.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **Extremely low win rate (16.9%)** with **103 losses vs. 21 wins**.  
- **Poor risk-reward ratio**: Avg. win = $3.00 vs. avg. loss = -$2.22.  
- Over-reliance on **7-pip stop-losses (SL)**, leading to frequent small losses.  
- No clear edge in entry logic (e.g., multiple SL hits in tight ranges).  

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Restrict trading to London session (07:00–12:00 UTC)** to focus on high-liquidity periods where some winning trades occurred.  
- **Increase stop-loss distance** (e.g., 15–20 pips) to avoid frequent SL triggers in choppy markets.  
- **Standardize lot sizing** (e.g., 0.05 lots flat) to simplify risk management.  
- **Filter out underperforming pairs** (e.g., pairs with consistent SL losses in the log).  

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **High losing streak risk**: 103 losses in 124 trades suggest poor entry timing.  
- **Negative expectancy**: With a profit factor <1 and low win rate, capital erosion is likely.  
- **Drawdown severity**: 16.6% max drawdown is unsustainable for most traders.  

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize further** with the above parameter tweaks, focusing on:  
1. Session filtering (London only).  
2. Adjusted SL/TP ratios (e.g., 15-pip SL, 20-pip TP).  
3. Pair performance analysis to exclude losers.  

If retesting shows no improvement, **disable the strategy**.
