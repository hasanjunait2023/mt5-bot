# Backtest Analysis: S5 — News Spike Strategy

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Unprofitable**  
The strategy generated a **-26.0% return** with a **profit factor of 0.39** (losses > gains) and a **max drawdown of 26.0%**, indicating consistent negative performance.

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- **Occasional high-pip wins**: 12 winning trades captured +70 pips each (e.g., $19.80–$22.20 gains).  
- **Defined risk-reward**: Fixed SL (-30 pips) and TP (+70 pips) structure suggests disciplined exit rules.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **Low win rate (21.4%)**: 44 losses vs. 12 wins, eroding capital.  
- **Negative expectancy**: Avg. loss (-$9.64) > avg. win ($13.65) in absolute value.  
- **Overtrading**: 56 trades in 5 months with minimal profit contribution from most trades.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Restrict trading sessions**: Focus on **London (07:00–12:00 UTC)** or **New York (12:00–17:00 UTC)** where volatility aligns with news releases.  
- **Adjust SL/TP**: Test **SL = -20 pips** and **TP = +50 pips** to improve risk-reward ratio (1:2.5 → 1:2.5).  
- **Filter trade setups**: Add volatility filters (e.g., ATR > X) or avoid trades during low-impact news.  
- **Reduce position size**: Cap lot size at **0.25–0.30** to lower per-trade risk.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **High drawdown risk**: 26% max drawdown on a $1,000 account is unsustainable.  
- **Liquidity risk**: Frequent SL triggers suggest poor entry timing during news spikes.  
- **Sharpe ratio**: Likely negative due to consistent losses and low returns.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize further** with:  
1. Session-based filtering (e.g., London only).  
2. Tighter SL/TP and reduced lot sizes.  
3. Backtest on additional news events or timeframes.  
**Do not deploy** until improvements in win rate and risk-adjusted returns are achieved.  

---

SUMMARY: Unprofitable strategy with 21.4% win rate and -26% return; optimize session timing, SL/TP, and position sizing before retesting.
