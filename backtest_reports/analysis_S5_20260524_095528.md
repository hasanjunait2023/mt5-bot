# Backtest Analysis: S5 — News Spike Strategy  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Unprofitable**  
The strategy generated a **-26.0% return** with a **profit factor of 0.39** (losses exceeding gains) and a **max drawdown of 26.0%**, indicating consistent negative performance.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- Occasional winning trades with TP2 hits (e.g., +70 pips gains).  
- Some consistency in small SL wins (e.g., repeated +3.30/$3.40 trades).  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **Low win rate (21.4%)** with **44 losses vs. 12 wins**.  
- **Negative expectancy**: Average loss (-$9.64) nearly negates average win (+$13.65).  
- High drawdown (26%) relative to capital at risk.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Restrict trading to London session (07:00–12:00 UTC)** to focus on high-liquidity periods where news impacts may be more predictable.  
- **Adjust risk-reward ratio**: Increase TP to 100–150 pips or reduce SL to 20 pips to improve profitability of winning trades.  
- **Filter news events**: Exclude low-impact or volatile news (e.g., surprise rate changes) that may cause erratic price spikes.  
- **Optimize lot sizing**: Use dynamic position sizing based on volatility (e.g., ATR-based) to reduce risk per trade.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **High risk of ruin**: 26% drawdown on a small sample of trades suggests poor risk management.  
- **Negative skew**: Strategy loses more frequently and more severely than it wins.  
- **Low reliability**: Profit factor <1 and win rate <25% indicate unsustainable edge.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Disable** the strategy in its current form. If further exploration is desired:  
- Run sensitivity analysis on SL/TP and session filters.  
- Backtest on a narrower time window (e.g., 2025–2026) to isolate performance trends.  

---

SUMMARY: Unprofitable strategy with 21.4% win rate, -26% return, and 26% drawdown; disable or re-optimize with tighter SL, session filters, and improved risk-reward ratios.
