# Backtest Analysis: S5 — News Spike Strategy  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Unprofitable**  
The strategy generated a **-26.0% return** with a **profit factor of 0.39** (losses exceed gains) and a **26.0% max drawdown**, indicating consistent negative performance.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- Occasional winning trades with **+70 pips** (e.g., May 2026 trades).  
- Some months showed minor profitability (e.g., April 2026: **+$9.90**, May 2026: **+$142.90**).  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **Low win rate (21.4%)** with **44 losses vs. 12 wins**.  
- **Lopsided risk-reward**: Avg. loss (**-$9.64**) > Avg. win (**+$13.65**).  
- High frequency of **stop-loss triggers** (-30 pips) without sufficient winning follow-through.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Restrict trading to London session (07:00–12:00 UTC)** to focus on high-liquidity periods where news impacts are most pronounced.  
- **Adjust stop-loss to 40–50 pips** to allow more room for news-driven volatility.  
- **Increase take-profit to 100 pips** to improve risk-reward ratio (currently ~1:2.33).  
- **Filter trades during low-volatility periods** (e.g., Asian session) to reduce false signals.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **High drawdown risk** (26% max) due to frequent losses and poor risk-reward.  
- **Overexposure to news events** without proper filtering, leading to inconsistent results.  
- **Leverage risk**: Even modest position sizing (0.33–0.38 lots) amplified losses.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize further** with the above parameter adjustments, then re-backtest. If results remain unprofitable, **disable** the strategy.
