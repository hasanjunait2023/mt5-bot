# Backtest Analysis: S6 — Asian Range  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable** — The strategy generates a +35.4% return with a profit factor of 1.21, indicating consistent edge despite a 19.7% max drawdown.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths:**  
- **Positive Return**: +$353.67 net profit over 2.5 years.  
- **Decent Win Rate**: 54.5% of trades are profitable.  
- **Balanced Risk-Reward**: Average win ($14.07) slightly offsets average loss ($13.93).  

**Weaknesses:**  
- **High Drawdown**: 19.7% max drawdown suggests significant risk exposure.  
- **Inconsistent Monthly Performance**: Multiple months with losses (e.g., -98.96 in 2025-09).  
- **Large Losing Trades**: Occasional big losses (e.g., -$31.20 on 2024-12-19).  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Restriction**: Trade only during **Asian session (21:00–03:00 UTC)** to align with the strategy’s design and avoid overlap with volatile European/Open sessions.  
- **Risk-Reward Adjustment**: Set **TP at 150 pips** and **SL at 100 pips** to improve profit factor (current avg. win/loss ~1:1).  
- **Filter News Events**: Avoid trading 1 hour before/after major economic releases to reduce unexpected slippage.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Moderate Risk Profile**: 19.7% drawdown is acceptable for a discretionary strategy but requires strict risk management.  
- **Liquidity Dependence**: Performance may degrade in low-liquidity periods (e.g., holidays).  
- **Overfitting Concern**: Strategy may require robust walk-forward testing to ensure out-of-sample validity.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize Further** — Test adjusted session hours, TP/SL ratios, and news filters. Validate robustness with Monte Carlo analysis before deployment.
