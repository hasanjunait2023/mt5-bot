# Backtest Analysis: S6 — Asian Range  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Marginal/Profitable**  
The strategy generates a **+35.4% return** with a **1.21 profit factor** and **54.5% win rate**, indicating edge. However, **19.7% max drawdown** and inconsistent monthly performance (e.g., -$98.96 in 2025-09) suggest reliability concerns.  

---

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  

**Strengths**:  
- **Positive expectancy**: Avg. win ($14.07) > Avg. loss ($13.93).  
- **Frequent small wins**: Many trades closed via TP2/ForceClose with modest gains.  
- **Periodic strong performance**: Months like 2025-01 (+$60.21) and 2025-05 (+$119.80) show potential.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- **High drawdown**: 19.7% peak-to-trough decline undermines consistency.  
- **Lumpy performance**: Multiple losing months (e.g., 2025-07: -$86.13) and large single-trade losses (e.g., -$31.20 on 2024-12-19).  
- **Overreliance on SL**: Frequent stop-loss triggers suggest poor risk management or volatile entries.  

---

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session restriction**: Trade only during **Asian session (22:00–06:00 UTC)** to align with low-volatility range-bound conditions.  
- **Risk per trade**: Reduce lot size to **0.005–0.01** to mitigate impact of large losses.  
- **Stop-loss adjustment**: Tighten SL to **50–70 pips** to avoid outsized losses (e.g., 953-pip loss on 2024-10-03).  
- **Take-profit refinement**: Use **dynamic TP** (e.g., trail stop) instead of fixed TP2 for better profit capture.  

---

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Drawdown risk**: 19.7% max drawdown is acceptable for high-risk strategies but may stress conservative portfolios.  
- **Liquidity risk**: Large SL losses suggest potential slippage during low-liquidity periods.  
- **Survivability**: Strategy survived 2+ years of testing but requires robust risk management to avoid ruin during drawdowns.  

---

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize further**  
Focus on:  
1. Reducing max drawdown via position sizing or session filtering.  
2. Improving SL/TP logic to minimize large losses.  
3. Validating performance on out-of-sample data before deployment.
