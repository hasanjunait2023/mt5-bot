# Backtest Analysis: S6v2 — Asian Range Strategy  

## 1. Overall Verdict  
**Profitable**  
The strategy generated a +43% return over 2.5 years with a profit factor of 1.41 and a max drawdown of 7.6%, indicating consistent profitability despite occasional losses.  

## 2. Key Strengths and Weaknesses  
**Strengths**:  
- High win rate (64.6%) demonstrates robust edge in identifying favorable setups.  
- Low max drawdown (7.6%) suggests effective risk management.  
- Strong profit factor (1.41) and positive equity curve.  

**Weaknesses**:  
- Larger average losses (-$16.48) compared to wins (+$12.77), eroding profits during drawdowns.  
- Inconsistent monthly performance (e.g., -19.94 in 2024-08, -31.28 in 2024-10).  
- Occasional large losses (e.g., -$23.87 in 2024-11-14) indicate vulnerability to adverse moves.  

## 3. Specific Parameter Recommendations  
- **Session Restriction**: Confine trading to **Asian session (22:00–06:00 UTC)** to avoid overlap with volatile European/North American sessions.  
- **Risk-Reward Adjustment**: Increase take-profit (TP) relative to stop-loss (SL) to **1:1.5–1:2** ratio (e.g., TP=50 pips, SL=33 pips) to offset larger average losses.  
- **Loss Mitigation**: Implement a **trailing stop** after 50% of TP is reached to lock in profits.  
- **Volatility Filter**: Avoid trading during low-liquidity periods (e.g., holidays) or high-impact news events.  

## 4. Risk Assessment  
- **Moderate Risk**: Max drawdown is acceptable, but large individual losses (-$23.87) pose concentration risk.  
- **Liquidity Risk**: Strategy may underperform during sudden volatility spikes (e.g., geopolitical events).  
- **Overfitting Risk**: Ensure robustness via walk-forward testing before live deployment.  

## 5. Recommended Next Action  
**Optimize Further**  
Refine SL/TP ratios, test session restrictions, and validate performance on out-of-sample data. Address large losses via trailing stops or tighter risk management.  

SUMMARY: S6v2 shows profitability (43% return, 1.41 PF) but needs optimization to reduce large losses and inconsistency; next step: refine parameters and test before deployment.
